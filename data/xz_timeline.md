# xz Utils takeover: key timeline facts

- **2021-10-29: Jia Tan's first xz-devel patch.** A small `.editorconfig` patch, followed in Nov–Dec 2021 by test-framework, reproducible-build and memory-limit patches. Source: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00512.html
- **2022-04-27 to 2022-06-22: pressure campaign from "Jigar Kumar" and "Dennis Ens".** Examples: "Patches spend years on this mailing list" (Apr 28), "Is XZ for Java still maintained?" (May 19), "Over 1 month and no closer to being merged" (May 27), "Progress will not happen until there is new maintainer" (Jun 7), "Why wait until 5.4.0 to change maintainer?" (Jun 14), "Why not pass on maintainership for XZ for C…" (Jun 21). Sources: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00557.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00562.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00565.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00566.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00568.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00569.html
- **2022-05-19 and 2022-06-08: Lasse Collin says he is overloaded.** He writes that Jia Tan "might have a bigger role in the future", mentions "longterm mental health issues", and calls XZ "an unpaid hobby project". Sources: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00563.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00567.html
- **2022-06-29: Jia Tan is described as "practically a co-maintainer already".** Lasse also writes that "some change in maintainership is already in progress at least for XZ Utils". Source: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00571.html
- **2023-01-11: Jia Tan announces a release (5.4.1) for the first time.** The same message announces that the primary Git repository has moved to GitHub. Source: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00634.html
- **2023-03-18 / 2023-05-04: Jia Tan takes over release signing.** Lasse writes that "Future release tarballs might be signed by Jia Tan". The 5.4.3 announcement, sent by Jia Tan, says the signing key "has changed to my key". Sources: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00635.html, https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00636.html
- **2024-02-24: XZ Utils 5.6.0 released by Jia Tan.** This is the first backdoored release tarball. Source: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00653.html
- **2024-03-09: XZ Utils 5.6.1 released by Jia Tan.** It also contains the backdoor; the disclosure notes that in 5.6.1 "the backdoor was further obfuscated". Sources: https://www.mail-archive.com/xz-devel@tukaani.org/msg00673.html, https://www.openwall.com/lists/oss-security/2024/03/29/4
- **2024-03-29: Andres Freund publicly discloses the backdoor on oss-security.** It is assigned CVE-2024-3094 (the post says "Red Hat assigned this issue CVE-2024-3094"). Source: https://www.openwall.com/lists/oss-security/2024/03/29/4
- **About a 500 ms SSH slowdown led to the discovery.** In the disclosure, a failing ssh login took `real 0m0.299s` before and `real 0m0.807s` after (about 0.5 s more). `sshd -h` took "about 0.5s" with the backdoor, against "about 0.01s" without it. Source: https://www.openwall.com/lists/oss-security/2024/03/29/4
